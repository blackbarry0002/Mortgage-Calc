Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the mortgage calculator?

Our calculator uses industry-standard formulas and produces results within $1 of actual calculations from major lenders. However, actual costs may vary based on specific lender requirements and market conditions.

What's included in the monthly payment calculation?

Our payment calculator includes principal and interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA fees. It does not include utilities, maintenance costs, PMI (in most cases), or other non-standard fees.

What interest rate should I use?

Interest rates change daily based on market conditions and individual factors like your credit score. We recommend checking current rates with lenders or mortgage brokers for the most accurate information.

How does the affordability calculator work?

The affordability calculator uses the standard 28/36 debt-to-income ratio. It allows up to 28% of your gross monthly income for housing costs and 36% for total debt payments to determine how much house you can afford.

What's break-even in the refinance calculator?

Break-even is the number of months it takes for your monthly savings to equal the refinancing costs. After this point, you start saving money overall.

Is my personal information kept private?

Yes. Your information is secure and will never be shared with third parties for marketing purposes without your consent. Review our Privacy Policy for more details.

Should I use the results for major financial decisions?

Our calculator is for educational purposes. Always consult with qualified professionals (mortgage lenders, financial advisors, tax professionals) before making major financial decisions.

Can I export my amortization schedule?

Yes. You can view and print the amortization schedule from your browser. We're working on CSV export functionality for future releases.
